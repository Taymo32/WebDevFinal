About Me

I’ve always loved the outdoors—whether it’s skiing fresh powder, fishing quiet streams, or backpacking through the mountains. Along the way, I’ve found a passion for capturing those moments on camera. I make adventure-style videos, cinematic wedding films, and can even shoot everything from engagements to couple sessions. Whether you need a photographer for a golden-hour shoot or a videographer to tell your wedding story, I’m all about creating something meaningful and cool.
